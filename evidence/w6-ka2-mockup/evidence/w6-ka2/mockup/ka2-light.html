1 Karaoke mode: Tap joins Auto split beside the Line | Karaoke switch.
Tap is a split button like Play around and Commit: the face turns tap mode on and off, the arrow holds its options. To make room at the default 640 px dock, Karaoke mode folds Auto-scroll, Sync with video and Speech into ⋯ (AU1's narrow fold, one step earlier). Line mode keeps AU1's row as it is.

2 Tap options: how a key makes a syllable, speed and latency.
Hold (the default): key-down starts the next syllable and key-up ends it, so the time between a release and the next press becomes a gap. Press: each press starts the next syllable and ends the one before (no gaps). The menu shows the current speed and latency.

4 Tapping: the bar shows the Line's syllables; the held one grows at the playhead.
The first press set the Line's start (red); tapped syllables fill the lane at the top, a release before the next press shows as a hatched gap. The old end stays dashed until the last release sets the new one. The display follows the playhead. Other audio hotkeys pause while tapping, except Stop.

5 Undo: Backspace takes back the last syllable and plays again from 2 s before it.
Here “no” was still held, so it is dropped with “mi”, and playback jumped back 2 s of song time and goes on at the same speed; tap “mi” again when it comes. Shift+Backspace takes back the whole Line and starts again 2 s before it. Taps on finished Lines go back with Ctrl+Z.

6 Line end: the last release commits the Line and the next one is ready at once.
Each finished Line is one undo step ("Tap timing"); playback keeps going, so a whole song can be tapped in one pass. A gap becomes an empty syllable ({\k5}); a gap under 30 ms joins the syllable before it. Taps are rounded to 10 ms. The Line's \k, \kf or \ko tags keep their kind.

7 Stop: Esc stops playback and keeps the Line's taps pending.
Resume (Space) plays again from 2 s before the next untapped syllable. Revert Line puts the Line back as it was. Turning Tap off, or Esc a second time, also reverts the unfinished Line; finished Lines stay committed.

8 Calibrate latency: a compact Window (DG1 frame), opened from the Tap menu.
One-time, saved with the profile. The value is the median of the counted presses after outliers; the spin box lets you type it too. The playhead already allows for the device's own latency, so this only measures you, and it holds when the device changes.

9 A Line with one syllable: tapping waits for it to be split.
Auto split runs KA1's splitter; the Karaoke bar's own split also works. With Hold or Press, a Line with one syllable would only get its start and end, so Tap asks first.
