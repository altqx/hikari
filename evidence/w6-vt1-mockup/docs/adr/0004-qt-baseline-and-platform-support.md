# Advance Qt and explicitly retire Windows 10 later

In the live planning session on 2026-09-27, altqx chose “Advance Qt; explicitly retire Windows 10 later” for [the platform-policy decision](https://github.com/altqx/hikari/issues/37), accepting Qt 6.11.2 now and Qt 6.12 stable after publication and qualification, then future Qt advancement. Because [Qt identifies 6.12 as its last Windows 10 version](https://doc.qt.io/qt-6/supported-platforms.html), Windows 10 remains initially first-class until an explicit later retirement decision and notice, with no invented date, shared Qt ceiling or separate maintenance branch as the default. The [platform policy](../qt/platform-policy.md) preserves Windows 11, current Ubuntu LTS and Fedora as future first-class targets and a possible macOS path, and requires named maintenance responsibility and release qualification without assuming paid Qt support or free extended-LTS patches.
