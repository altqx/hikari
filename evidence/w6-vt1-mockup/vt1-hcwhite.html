Reading the bar. The tool's icon and name lead. Options are grouped under a short label. Number fields sit last, before the trailing target slot: Pick selected lines, how many are picked, and Clear.
Toggles are icons with their legacy help text on hover; a greyed icon is unavailable in the current mode. Choices show the current value as text (alignment, shape, tag, change option) rather than hide it behind an icon menu.
Number fields: drag the dotted label sideways to scrub (Shift for 0.1), or type and press Enter. A drag or an Enter is one undo step, and Esc during a drag cancels it. A field with no target (the Line is not on the video) is greyed.
Always there. The bar keeps its height for every tool, including the crosshair, so the video never resizes when you change tools or the pointer moves. The read-only readout beside the video times is gone; its values are here.
