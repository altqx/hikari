FR1 Getting started, version 2: Light (1 of 2)

Closer to MuseScore 4’s first-launch setup: a compact window, page dots on top, a centred title and explanation, Back on the left, Next and Finish on the right. Every choice applies at Finish; Skip setup, Close and Esc keep the defaults and import nothing. The legacy-upgrade run has 8 pages, a fresh install 7 (no Import).
