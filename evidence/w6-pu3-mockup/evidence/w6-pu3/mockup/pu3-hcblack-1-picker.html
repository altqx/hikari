PU3 font picker (High contrast black)

One window on DG1’s frame replaces FontDialog.qml. The Line editor’s Font selection button and hotkey open it, modal to the main window, as today. Each change is applied to the Line at once (legacy FONT_CHANGED) and Cancel takes every change back. Fonts, catalogs and text are examples.

Search filters the list as you type (Ctrl+F; Up and Down move in the list while the field keeps the keyboard). Each family is drawn in its own face (SY3’s rows); its catalogs are muted at the right. The catalog is a chip (All fonts, Without catalog or one catalog) and the character filter is a second chip. The preview is the Line’s text, the selected part if there is one, drawn by the subtitle renderer, with the face it picked under it. Manage catalogs… opens that window on its own; it is never inside this one.
