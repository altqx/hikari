The catalogs on the left with their sizes, one icon strip (New, Rename, Delete; Load from a file, Add fonts from subtitles). On the right every installed font: the check says whether it is in the chosen catalog (Space toggles every selected row), with its catalogs and a sample. This replaces legacy’s mark column and per-row catalog menu. Show narrows the list; Refresh fonts reads the fonts again. The preview is the renderer’s. Edits save themselves (legacy’s autosave): the footer says Saved, the file and folder in its tooltip, instead of a sentence.

Add fonts from subtitles: a compact DG1 window (it opens from a menu). The catalog is chosen or typed (a new name makes it). “From” replaces “Add fonts from all open subtitles”; “Replace the catalog’s fonts” replaces “Remove all contents of catalog”. The verb button replaces OK. The result is a toast and the picker shows that catalog.
