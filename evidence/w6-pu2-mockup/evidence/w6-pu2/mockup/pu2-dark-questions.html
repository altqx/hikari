The find/replace question (one in-window dialog with five personalities) becomes PK1 message boxes with verb buttons; its OK-only messages become toasts. The translation mode prompts and the line group refusal too. Platform order as on Windows and KDE; the default is filled, a destructive verb outlined in the danger colour and never the default.
