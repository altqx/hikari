Loading from an MKV (window): replaces the modal progress dialog (“Loading subtitles from Matroska.”, the gauge, Time elapsed, Cancel). The loaded subtitles replace the tab’s when they are complete. The track chooser before it is already PK1’s list chooser; “The file does not contain any subtitle tracks.” becomes a toast.
