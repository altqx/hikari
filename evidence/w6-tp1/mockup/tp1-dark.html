2Styles is a new panel: the Document's Styles, applied with one key.
Each row shows the Style drawn in its own font and colours, its font and size, and how many Lines use it. The pencil marks the current Line's Style, which follows the editing target. Enter or a double-click applies the chosen Style to the selected Lines (one undo step); the header holds Apply, New, Edit, Duplicate, Delete and Style manager, names on hover. Typing filters. The row menu (right-click, Menu key) adds Select Lines with this Style and Copy to the catalog. Catalogs and the full Style editor stay in the Style manager window (SY1).

6Questions for the owner, and notes
  1. Where Styles docks: right of the video (A, recommended) or left of it (B).
  2. Below the video: Line editor and Grid side by side (A, recommended) or stacked full width (C).
  3. Styles panel content: the Document's Styles only, catalogs and the Style editor staying in the Style manager window (recommended), or the catalog list in the panel too.
  4. Enter / double-click on a Style: applies it to the selected Lines, as a MuseScore palette applies to the selection (recommended), or opens the Style editor.

What changes. Typesetting stops being Editing minus Audio: the preset builds its own arrangement. Timing, Translation and Editing are unchanged.

Saved arrangements. A tab, Session, file or saved layout that already holds a Typesetting arrangement keeps its own panels; Reset this layout and choosing Typesetting again give the new one.

The Styles panel is in View > Panels in every arrangement, closed in the others. Like every panel it can be moved, tabbed or floated; it follows the editing Document (WS pin rules apply).

Translation mode on: the Original text panel takes its usual place above the Line editor (TM1).

Video rows: Seek on / Then play are not drawn: VF1 (#331) moves them to the Video panel's ⋯ menu.
