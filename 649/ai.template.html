Nothing AI-related shows, downloads, starts or reads your keychain. Providers, keys and threads are kept, and turning AI back on brings them back without a restart.

Kept while AI is off

  • 4 providers, and their keys in the keychain
  • Your agent threads
  • The downloaded AI component (55 MB)

Hidden while AI is off

  • The agent panel, inline AI actions and AI rows in every menu
  • AI commands in command search, their shortcuts and toolbar buttons
  • AI in Getting started and What’s new
  • The MCP server, which stops listening
