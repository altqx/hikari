Where they open

Shift times: Subtitles › Shift times… (Ctrl+I), placed by DK2’s rule. Timing postprocessor: Timing › Timing postprocessor…, docked beside the Audio panel in the Timing layout and closed in the others.

Keyboard

Ctrl+Enter applies from any field. The Shift times hotkey (5300) still shifts with the remembered values without opening the panel. Esc goes back to the Grid.

Screen readers

The preview’s first line is a live status (“Changes 31 of 34 lines”). Each row reads its line, the old and new start and end, and the reason.
