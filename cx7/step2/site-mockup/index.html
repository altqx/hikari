If you know Aegisub

  • Files: your ASS files open and save as they are, and lines other programs added are kept.
  • Styles: the same styles; Aegisub’s storages are catalogues here.
  • Tags: the same ASS tags, drawn with libass.
  • Timing keys: play, commit and next line work in the Audio panel and the Grid.
  • Scripts: your Lua and MoonScript macros and DependencyControl run.

Coming from Aegisub has the full table, including the 54 features that aren’t there yet.

Coming from Aegisub

What’s the same, what’s better, and what isn’t in HikariSub yet, feature by feature against Aegisub 3.5.

We checked HikariSub against every command, window and option in Aegisub 3.5 and the fork’s additions: 173 features. Most work the same or better. The ones that aren’t here yet are named below, in each area, and this page changes as they arrive.

Everything you do in Aegisub, plus projects, several windows and an AI that only changes what you keep. The subtitle editor for typesetting, timing, karaoke and translation, with projects, several windows and an AI that only changes what you keep.

Everything you do in Aegisub

Better:

  • Waveform and spectrum display: a Speech spectrum mode.
  • Play selection, line, toggle, stop; 500 ms before, after, first, last; to the end: also play around a marker, and slower playback at 25 to 75% with the pitch kept.

  • Snap start or end to the video frame: a joined neighbour stays joined.
  • Shift the selection so the active line starts at the current frame: also end at video, and all following lines.
  • Shift times by time or frames, forward or back, chosen lines and times: live preview, shift to the video or an audio mark, move tag times, centre within frames, presets.
