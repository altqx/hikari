# T6: the Position shifter and the all-tags tool (visual_t6_capture.cpp).
# The video rectangle is a 1280x720 frame in a 640x400 client (40-pixel
# panel): (0, 0, 640, 360), the script 1920x1080, so the tools' coefficients
# are 3 and the Line at \pos(960,540) sits at (320, 180).
#
# "frame" (the decoded frame: width, height, SAR) and "viewzoom" (a wheel
# zoom: percent, x, y) are the rewrite's (its VideoView makes the rectangle
# and the zoom from them); the probe takes "rect" and "zoom" as legacy's
# RendererVideo gave them for the same, as the T1 capture
# (local-t1-visual-20261005) records.
#
# The all-tags sliders sit 30 pixels in from either side of the video
# (30-610), the first with its top at 34 and bottom at 40, the next 70 lower.
# A value v of a tag ranging min-max is at 30 + (v - min) * 580 / (max - min);
# the thumb takes 4 pixels either side and 10 above and below the slider.
