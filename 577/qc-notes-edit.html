4 · What goes into the .ass. One [Script Info] key per note, in time order. libass ignores unknown keys, and Aegisub keeps them when it saves.

[Script Info] ScriptType: v4.00+ PlayResX: 1920 PlayResY: 1080 … Hikari QC 1: 0:00:41.00,General,done,OP karaoke is missing \be on the romaji. Hikari QC 2: 0:02:11.00,TLC,open,"Is anyone there?" → "Hello? Anyone?"\Nreads more naturally. Hikari QC 3: 0:03:24.00,TLC,open,yabai should be translated as "terrible" instead. Hikari QC 4: 0:03:24.40,Timing,open,Line 57 flashes for one frame at the cut … [V4+ Styles] …

Fields: time, section, open or done, then the text. Line breaks are written as \N. Section names can’t contain commas; import turns them into spaces.
