## Routing and closure rules

- **A — authoritative:** apply an accepted contract or preserve characterized legacy behavior. Internal implementation details do not need a vote. Unknown legacy behavior requires investigation/fixtures, not a preference question to the user.
- **B — shared decision:** one owning ticket decides the policy; surfaces consume its recorded outcome. Do not create separate Audio, Editor, Search and Timing draft policies accidentally.
- **C — surface choice/departure:** retain only a genuinely undefined interaction or a specifically desired observable change. A harmless alternative demonstrated in a prototype can be retired in favor of required compatibility without approving it. Suspected data loss/crashes are different: preserve evidence and obtain a named disposition, not an automatic instruction to reproduce the hazard.
- **D — implementation/evidence:** name the exact operation, fixture and native observation required. This is neither another human preference nor proof already obtained.

A prototype's closure record should name its accepted layout, A rules, B owners, disposition of any remaining C items and D obligations with accountable downstream ownership. It must not merely say “remaining parity.” Production qualification can transfer explicitly to implementation capabilities under [Gate 0](implementation-sequence.md#gate-0-finish-the-specification-and-feasibility-decisions) and the [testing contract](../testing.md); design-critical failed/inconclusive feasibility stays with its existing native ticket. This worksheet neither closes tickets nor declares those blockers passed. No layout response approves mandatory confirmation before every operation, sample history limits, fixture-only refusals or a changed parser.
